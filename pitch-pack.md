## 5. Loom script (60–90 seconds)

- **0:00–0:10** — "This is the tracker an accounting firm uses. These clients owe documents. Watch what happens without the accountant touching anything."
- **0:10–0:30** — Run the workflow. Show rows being picked up by stage. "Brightline gets a first reminder. Cornerstone gets the firmer second one. Northwind gets the final notice."
- **0:30–0:45** — Open the sent emails. "Each one is logged here, and the tracker updated itself: stage, date, next follow-up."
- **0:45–1:00** — Show Atlas. "Three reminders, no reply. The system stops emailing and escalates to the accountant with a summary."
- **1:00–1:15** — Break it: run with a bad email address. "And when something fails, the owner gets an alert and the failure is logged. Nothing dies silently."
- **1:15–1:30** — "I install this for firms and monitor it monthly. Links and details below."

## 6. Tracking your outreach

Log every pitch: firm, contact, date, angle used (setup/internship), reply, outcome. 20–30 pitches minimum before judging the lane. Silence is data about the pitch, not about you.
